About PawStay

A warm hand for your pets

Built on trust, reliability, and genuine love for animals — PawStay treats your pets like family.

PawStay Pet Sitting is a local pet care service offering in-home visits, daily walks, and overnight stays for dogs, cats, and small pets. Whether you're traveling, working late, or just need a helping hand — we're here.

Every sitter is vetted, pet-first, and trained to handle different temperaments and needs. We send you regular photo updates and detailed reports, so you always know how your pet is doing — even when you're miles away.

No cages, no kennels, no stress. Just warm, personal care in the comfort of your pet's own home.
