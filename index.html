About PawStay

A warm hand for your pets

Built on trust, reliability, and genuine love for animals — PawStay treats your pets like family.

PawStay Pet Sitting is a local pet care service offering in-home visits, daily walks, and overnight stays for dogs, cats, and small pets. Whether you're traveling, working late, or just need a helping hand — we're here.

Every sitter is vetted, pet-first, and trained to handle different temperaments and needs. We send you regular photo updates and detailed reports, so you always know how your pet is doing — even when you're miles away.

No cages, no kennels, no stress. Just warm, personal care in the comfort of your pet's own home.

FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Everything you need to know before booking your first visit.

Absolutely. Before your first booking, we schedule a free meet-and-greet so you, your pet, and your assigned sitter can get to know each other. This helps your pet feel comfortable and lets us understand their routine.

Yes. Our sitters are trained to give oral medications, topical treatments, and insulin injections (with proper instruction from you). We'll ask for complete dosage details during the meet-and-greet.

We'll contact you immediately and, if needed, take your pet to your preferred veterinarian. Please provide your vet's contact details during booking. For emergencies, we'll use the nearest 24-hour animal clinic.

We recommend booking at least 3–5 days in advance for regular visits, and 1–2 weeks in advance for overnight stays or holiday periods. Same-day bookings may be accommodated depending on availability.

Yes — we offer 10% off for each additional pet in the same household. Just mention it when you book and we'll apply the discount automatically.

After we confirm your booking and send you a fee summary, you can pay via GCash, Maya, Bank Transfer, or Cash. We'll send the exact payment details — account number, GCash number, or bank info — along with your confirmation so you know exactly where to send your payment.

We require a 50% downpayment to confirm and reserve your slot. The remaining balance is settled after the service is completed — once we've sent you the daily photo updates and completion report. No payment is required for cancellations made before the booking starts.

Yes — cash on completion is accepted for clients who prefer to pay in person. This is ideal for regular weekly walks or drop-in visits where the sitter meets you directly. For overnight or multi-day bookings, we recommend GCash, Maya, or bank transfer for convenience and record-keeping.
